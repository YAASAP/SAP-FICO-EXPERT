Privacy Policy

SAP FICO EXPERT app and website · Last updated: November 15, 2026
In short: the iPhone app does not collect any personal data. On the website, we only collect your e-mail address if you give it to us voluntarily (sign-up, contact, lost codes). No advertising trackers, no tracking. Access codes are stored only on your device.
Publisher

SAP FICO EXPERT is published by YAASAP, a French single-shareholder simplified joint-stock company, SIREN 101 085 462, Bordeaux trade register, Eysines (France). Representative: Yacine Aouabed.

Data collected

iPhone app: it requires no account, name, e-mail address or location, and does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera. No analytics, advertising or cross-app tracking tool is integrated.

Website: no data is requested to read the notes. Only three optional forms collect data, described below under “Website forms”.

Data stored on your device
  • Access code : if you have an access code for premium notes, it is saved in your device's local storage to unlock the notes. It is not sent to any publisher server.
  • Favorites (mobile app): the list of notes you mark as favorites is kept locally on your device.
  • Personal notes and recent notes (mobile app): the notes you write on a note and the list of recently viewed notes are kept locally on your device and never transmitted.
  • Offline mode : pages you have already viewed may be cached on the device to display without a connection.

You can erase this data at any time by deleting the app or clearing the site data in your browser.

Payments

Payments are not processed by the publisher. An in-app purchase in the iOS app is handled by Apple (App Store) under its own terms and privacy policy. The app checks locally with Apple that the Premium purchase is valid; the publisher receives no personal data related to this purchase. Any purchase made on the website goes through Stripe, which processes payment data directly; the publisher neither receives nor stores your card number.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and by Replit (Replit, Inc.) for the web app version and its forms; form data is stored in this host's database. Like any host, it may record technical logs (IP address, date, requested page) to ensure security and operation of the service. The publisher has no access to these logs and does not use them.

The site pages load their fonts from Google Fonts : Google may receive your IP address in doing so, without the publisher having access to it.

Website forms

Sign-up for new notes: e-mail address, date and proof of your consent. Purpose: to send you YAASAP's new notes and offers. Legal basis: your consent, which can be withdrawn at any time (unsubscribe link or simple request). Retention: until you unsubscribe, or 3 years without interaction.

Contact form: name, e-mail address, subject and message. Purpose: to answer your request. Legal basis: your request (pre-contractual measures or legitimate interest). Retention: 3 years after the last exchange.

Lost codes: purchase e-mail address, product concerned and optional message. Purpose: to verify your purchase and resend your codes. Legal basis: performance of the sales contract. Retention: as long as needed for processing, then according to accounting obligations for proof of purchase. No access code is stored by the form.

This data is neither sold nor transferred. It is accessible only to the publisher and its technical providers (hosting, e-mail sending), within the limits of their mission. An IP address may be used temporarily to block abusive submissions.

Children

The content is aimed at finance and SAP professionals. The app does not knowingly collect any data concerning children.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to and restrict the processing of your data. The iPhone app does not collect any personal data; these rights therefore apply to data submitted through the website forms, by writing to us via the contact form. You may also contact the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to the publisher via the site's home page: yaasap.github.io/SAP-FICO-EXPERT.

Changes

This policy may change, for example when a new feature is added. The update date appears at the top of this page.
