  • SAP FIFS00 : Reconciliation accountA reconciliation account links a subledger (customers, vendors, fixed assets) to the general ledger. It is set in the account's control data at company-code level.
  • SAP FIFB50 : G/L account document entryFB50 posts a document to the general ledger: accrual, provision, reclassification. One single screen for all lines, with a mandatory zero balance.
  • SAP FIFB60 : Vendor invoiceFB60 posts a vendor invoice outside the logistics flow: overheads, fees, subscriptions. The vendor line and expense lines are entered on the same screen.
  • SAP FIFB70 : Customer invoiceFB70 posts a customer invoice directly in accounting, without going through sales billing. Ideal for re-invoicing or revenue outside the sales flow.
  • SAP FIF-53 : Manual outgoing paymentF-53 posts a manual vendor payment and clears the selected invoices. Use it for settlements outside the automatic payment run.
  • SAP FIF-28 : Incoming paymentF-28 posts a customer receipt and clears it against open invoices. The key decision is partial payment versus residual item.
  • SAP FIFBL1N : Vendor line itemsFBL1N displays a vendor's line items: open, cleared or all. It is the reference screen to track vendor payables and prepare a reconciliation.
  • SAP FIFBL5N : Customer line itemsFBL5N is the customer equivalent of FBL1N: open invoices, receipts and clearings. Essential before any dunning or unpaid-invoice analysis.
  • SAP FIFAGLL03 : G/L account line itemsFAGLL03 displays the lines of a general ledger account, with ledger selection. It is the control screen for expense, revenue and balance sheet accounts.
  • SAP FIFBRA : Reset cleared itemsFBRA reverses the clearing of a payment or account clearing. It puts the items back to open, without reversing the payment document itself.
  • SAP FIFB08 : Reverse documentFB08 reverses an accounting document by generating a reversal document. The original stays in the system, linked to its reversal.
  • SAP FIF.01 : Financial statementsF.01 outputs the balance sheet and income statement from a financial statement version. It is the most used summary view to check a closing.
  • SAP FIFAGLGVTR : Balance carryforwardFAGLGVTR carries balances forward to the new fiscal year: balance sheet to balance sheet accounts, profit/loss to the retained earnings account.
  • SAP FIFAGL_FCV : Foreign currency valuationFAGL_FCV revalues foreign-currency items at the closing date and posts unrealized exchange differences, with automatic reversal in the next period.
  • SAP FIF110 : Payment proposalF110 runs the automatic payment program: parameters, proposal, edit, execution and printout. It settles hundreds of invoices at once.
  • SAP FIFB03 : Display documentFB03 displays an accounting document with its lines, currency and header data. It is the read transaction you use most often to check an entry.
  • SAP FIFBV0 : Parked documentFBV0 lets you resume, complete, validate and post a parked document. A parked document has no effect on balances until it is posted.
  • SAP FIFBD1 : Recurring documentFBD1 creates a model document that is posted automatically at regular intervals: rent, subscription, insurance. It is executed by the recurring entry program.
  • SAP FIFB65 : Vendor credit memoFB65 posts a credit memo received from a vendor. It creates a credit on the vendor item that must be cleared against the invoice or deducted from the next payment.
  • SAP FIFD32 : Customer credit limitFD32 changes a customer's credit management data: credit control area, limit, risk category. It blocks or warns on orders exceeding the exposure.
  • SAP FIFK01 / XK01 : Vendor creationFK01 creates a vendor on the accounting side, XK01 creates it centrally (accounting and purchasing). In S/4HANA, creation goes through the business partner.
  • SAP FIFD01 : Customer creationFD01 creates a customer on the accounting side. It carries the reconciliation account and payment terms; sales data is completed afterwards through sales transactions.
  • SAP MM-FIMIGO : Goods receipt 101MIGO records the goods receipt for a purchase order with movement type 101. It creates the stock document and the accounting document at the same time, with the GR/IR account as offset.
  • SAP MM-FIMIRO : Logistics invoiceMIRO records a vendor invoice against a purchase order or goods receipt. It clears the GR/IR account and records price differences.
  • SAP MM-FIMR11 : GR/IR account clearingMR11 clears the GR/IR balances that remain open: goods received not invoiced, or invoices received without receipt. Used at closing after analysis.
  • SAP FIF-03 : Clear G/L accountF-03 clears the open items of an open-item-managed G/L account. It is used to clean up suspense or clearing accounts.
  • Fixed assetsABZON : Asset acquisitionABZON posts a fixed asset acquisition with automatic offsetting entry. Used when the asset is created without a matching vendor invoice.
  • Fixed assetsABAVN : Asset scrappingABAVN posts the retirement of an asset without sale proceeds: breakage, destruction, theft. The net book value is recognized as a loss.
  • Fixed assetsAW01N : Asset explorerAW01N displays an asset's values by depreciation area and fiscal year. It is the control screen for acquisition value, depreciation and net book value.
  • SAP FIKSB1 : Cost center line itemsKSB1 displays the lines posted to a cost center: actual costs by cost element. It is the basic screen to justify a budget variance.
  • SAP FIOBD4 : G/L account groupsThe G/L account group sets an account's number range and the layout of its screen fields. It is defined per chart of accounts with OBD4.
  • SAP FIOB52 : Open posting periodsOB52 opens and closes posting periods by account type, from a posting period variant shared across company codes. It is the first reflex when a posting is rejected.
  • SAP FIOBA4 : Employee tolerancesOBA4 defines employee tolerance groups: maximum posting amounts and accepted differences. It does not handle company customer and vendor tolerances (OBA3).
  • SAP FIOBA7 : Document typesThe document type distinguishes transactions and drives filing. It carries the number range, allowed account types and reversal type.
  • SAP FIOBC4 : Field status variantThe field status variant groups field status groups that make G/L entry fields optional, required or hidden. It is defined with OBC4 and assigned to the company code with OBC5.
  • SAP FIFBN1 : Document number rangesFBN1 maintains accounting document number intervals, by company code and fiscal year. Each document type points to a range key, with internal or external numbering.
  • SAP FIFINSC_LEDGER : Company code currenciesIn S/4HANA, a company code's currencies are configured per ledger with currency types in FINSC_LEDGER. You choose the parallel currencies stored in ACDOCA.
  • SAP FIFTXP : Tax codesFTXP creates and maintains 2-character tax codes, per country calculation procedure, with their tax type and rate. Tax accounts are configured separately, in OB40.
  • SAP FIOB08 : Exchange ratesOB08 is used to enter exchange rates by rate type and validity date. Direct or indirect quotation and the inverse rate are set up beforehand.
  • SAP FIOBA1 : Exchange rate differencesOBA1 determines the exchange rate difference accounts (KDB, KDF) and revaluation accounts. All accounts must be filled in, otherwise foreign currency valuation fails.
  • SAP FIFINSC_LEDGER : LedgersFINSC_LEDGER defines the ledgers and their currency types. The leading ledger 0L is unique and mandatory; non-leading ledgers are optional.
  • SAP FIOBYA : Intercompany postingsOBYA configures the clearing accounts between company codes of the same group for intercompany postings. It is used with OBY6.
  • SAP FIOBB8 : Payment termsOBB8 defines payment terms: baseline date, discount tiers, fixed day and additional months. They are then assigned to customer and vendor records.
  • SAP FIOBA3 : Customer and vendor tolerancesOBA3 defines, per company code, the settlement differences SAP clears automatically when clearing a customer or vendor: amount, percentage and discount adjustment.
  • SAP FIFBMP : Dunning procedureFBMP maintains the dunning procedure: levels, days in arrears, grace periods, charges, minimum amounts and forms. Dunning runs are then executed with F150.
  • SAP FIOB46 : Interest calculationOB46 defines the interest calculation indicator: P for interest on open items, S for interest on balances. It is completed by rates, terms and accounts.
  • SAP FIFBZP : Payment program configurationFBZP is the central configuration screen of payment program F110. You go through it from top to bottom: company codes, paying company codes, payment methods, then bank determination.
  • SAP FIOBXL : Settlement differencesOBXL assigns the G/L account that receives customer and vendor settlement differences. It is used with OBA3 (tolerances) and OBBE (difference reasons).
  • SAP FIOB74 : Automatic clearingOB74 prepares automatic clearing: it defines the grouping criteria for the open items of an account. Program F.13 then applies them to clear.
  • Fixed assetsEC08 : Depreciation chartThe chart of depreciation groups a country's depreciation areas. You create it by copying the SAP reference chart with EC08, then assign it to the company code with OAOB.
  • Fixed assetsOAOA : Asset classesThe asset class defines the structure of the asset master record: screen layout, number range, account determination and depreciation defaults. You define it with OAOA.
  • Fixed assetsAFAMA : Depreciation keysThe depreciation key defines how SAP calculates depreciation: base method, declining balance, multilevel, period control and rounding. It can combine several phases.
  • Fixed assetsAO90 : Asset accounting G/L accountsAO90 assigns G/L accounts to FI-AA transactions: acquisition, depreciation, disposal. The assignment depends on the account determination key, area and transaction type.
  • Fixed assetsOAYR : Posting intervalsOAYR sets the intervals and rules for posting depreciation to the general ledger. OAYZ activates areas per class, AO71 defines the document type.
  • Fixed assetsIMG types de mouvement : Asset transaction typesTransaction types classify FI-AA operations: acquisitions, retirements, transfers. You create new ones by copying a standard type, using customer-reserved keys.
  • SAP COOKKP : Controlling areaThe controlling area is the organizational unit of controlling. OKKP sets its currency, chart of accounts, fiscal year variant, company code assignment mode and standard hierarchy.
  • SAP COOKEON : Standard cost center hierarchyThe standard hierarchy is the tree that groups all cost centers of a controlling area. OKEON lets you build and check it before creating the cost centers.
  • SAP COKA01 : Cost elementsIn S/4HANA, a cost element is a general ledger account. Its role in CO is set in the control data of FS00: account type and category.
  • SAP COKSV5 : Distribution cyclesKSV5 runs actual cost distribution cycles between cost centers. The cycle is defined in KSV1; its assessment counterpart goes through KSU1 and KSU5.
  • SAP COKOT2_OPA : Internal order typesThe order type sets the behavior of an internal order: settlement profile, number range, planning profile. KOT2_OPA is the maintenance transaction for these types.
  • SAP COOKB9 : Automatic CO account assignmentOKB9 provides a default CO account assignment for primary cost accounts when a system-generated posting has no CO object: stock movements, invoices, SD billing.
  • SAP FIOX02 · OX15 · OB13 · OB29 · OBBO · OBC4 : FI enterprise structure and variantsClient, company code, chart of accounts and three variants (fiscal year, open periods, field status) form the foundation of any SAP accounting. You define them once, then assign them to the company code.
  • SAP FIOBA7 · FBN1 · OB41 · OBC4 · OB32 : Accounting documents: types, numbers, keysFI document control relies on the document type, its number range, the posting key and the field status. These four parameters determine what the user can enter and how the document is numbered.
  • SAP FIOBA4 · OBA3 · OBA0 · OB52 · OB07 · OB08 : Tolerances, periods, currencies, ratesTolerances limit amounts and clearing differences, OB52 opens or closes periods, and currency types, rate types and exchange rates drive conversions. You set them together because they condition data entry.
  • SAP FIOBD4 · FS00 · FSP0 · FSS0 : G/L accounts and chart of accountsThe chart of accounts (client level) holds the G/L accounts; the company code segment specifies their accounting use. OBD4 account groups drive numbering and field statuses; the S/4HANA account type replaces cost elements.
  • SAP FIFINSC_LEDGER · OB29 · OBBO : Ledgers, document splitting and segmentsThe leading ledger 0L and non-leading ledgers carry parallel accounting through accounting principles. Document splitting and segments provide complete financial statements by segment, profit center or other characteristic.

  • SAP FIOBD3 · XKN1 · OBAS · BP · OBB8 : Vendors: master dataVendor account groups (OBD3), their number ranges (XKN1, OBAS), payment terms and the reconciliation account structure each record. In S/4HANA, creation goes through the Business Partner and CVI.
  • SAP FIOBYR · OBA3 · OB27 · OB74 : Vendor operations: down payments, differences, blocksDown payments and special items, difference tolerances (OBA3), difference reasons, payment blocks (OB27), withholding tax, parking, bills of exchange and clearing.   • SAP FIOBD2 · XDN1 · OBAR · BP · FS00 : Customers: master data and BPA customer relies on its account group, number range and reconciliation account. In S/4HANA, creation goes through the Business Partner and the CVI integration, which must stay aligned with the FI configuration.
